elfyou Extension Privacy Notice

Last updated 22 September 2026

The extension collects no data about you. It does not read chat, does not ask for your Twitch identity, stores nothing in your browser, and makes no requests to anyone except to load its own files.

This notice covers the elfyou Twitch extension, which shows e.l.f. Cosmetics products in a panel below the stream and in an overlay on top of the video. It is published by DenwaVR.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not connect to Twitch chat or read any chat messages.
  • It does not request access to your Twitch identity, and never sees your Twitch username or account details.
  • It stores nothing in your browser: no cookies, no local storage, no other browser storage. Nothing you do in the extension is remembered between sessions.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code, and sends no data about you anywhere.

What the extension loads

The product list and product images are part of the extension itself and are served by Twitch along with it. The extension does not fetch anything from any other server, so no third party is told that you opened it or which products you looked at.

Twitch

Twitch requires every extension to load its Extension Helper script. That script belongs to Twitch, and Twitch's handling of extension use is covered by Twitch's privacy notice. This extension does not use the helper to request any information about you.

Links to Amazon

Some products have a "Shop on Amazon" button that opens the product's Amazon listing in a new tab. Nothing is sent to Amazon when a product is shown. Amazon only receives your visit if you choose to open the link.

Some of these links carry Amazon Attribution parameters supplied by Amazon (for example maas, ref_=aa_maas and tag=maas). They let e.l.f. count how many visits to a listing came from the extension. They are part of the link itself, and are only read by Amazon once you open it. From then on, your visit is covered by Amazon's privacy notice, not this one.

Deals

During a scheduled promotion, products on offer are marked and shown in a Deals section. Whether a deal is running is worked out from the clock on your own device. The check happens in your browser, and the time is not sent anywhere.

Changes to this notice

If the extension changes in a way that affects your data, this notice will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
